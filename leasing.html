FAQ

Questions owners ask us

We have paper leases already. Do we start over?

No. We scan your current leases, and the AI pulls out the term, rent, escalation and deposit for each unit. New leases are then signed digitally.

Can tenants still pay by check or PDC?

Yes. PDCs are logged with their dates and bank, and you get a reminder before each deposit date. GCash and bank transfer are added as easier options.

How do you handle 5% withholding tax from commercial tenants?

Each bill shows the gross rent and the expected withholding. When a tenant pays the net amount, the system notes the 2307 still due, and tenants can upload it in the portal.

Do brokers get access?

Brokers can see vacant units and book viewings for their clients. Their referrals and commissions are tracked, without access to your tenants’ data.

We manage apartments, not commercial units. Does it fit?

Yes. The same steps work for apartments, condos, boarding houses and dorms: listings, viewings, leases, monthly billing, repairs and renewals.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks for Starter and four weeks for a full building, including loading your units, tenants and current leases, and training your staff.
