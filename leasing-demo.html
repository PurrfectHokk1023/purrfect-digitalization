    Inquiries & viewings

    Messages from Facebook, the website and QR signs. The AI drafts a reply from the live unit board; you send it and offer viewing slots.

    Applications & leases

    Prospects who viewed get an application link. When documents are complete, approve to create the lease from the template, then send it for e-signature.

    Billing · November 2026

    One bill per occupied unit: rent, CUSA and electricity by submeter. Commercial tenants withhold 5% EWT on rent and owe you a BIR Form 2307.

    Repairs

    Tenants report by QR or the portal. The AI sets urgency and suggests who should fix it; the fix is closed with a photo.

    Renewals

    Leases ending within 90 days of the demo date. The AI suggests the new rent from the lease’s 5% escalation and the tenant’s payment record.
