      FAQ

      Questions shop owners ask

      Is there a monthly fee?

      No. You pay once (₱179₱299) and keep using it, with free updates.

      Until when is the 40% off?

      Until 11:59 PM on December 31, 2026 (Manila time). Pay before then and it’s ₱179 instead of ₱299, with the same license and free updates.

      Can I try it before paying?

      Yes. The free demo is the full app with sample items; receipts just print “DEMO”. Add your own items there, on the device you’ll sell on: they stay when you activate. On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first and set up in that icon.

      How soon do I get my license key?

      Right away when the page verifies your GCash receipt: it comes by email within a minute. If it can’t verify the receipt (or you send it for a manual check), we check it by hand, usually within a few hours between 8 AM and 10 PM.

      Can I check my shop when I’m not there?

      Yes. Turn on Online back office in the register’s Settings, and open your back office on any phone: today’s sales, net profit (sales minus your item costs and expenses), low stock and, with Pro, who is at work. Only you can open it, from the register or with a link we email you. Off, nothing leaves the register.

      What if I paid the wrong amount?

      Add your GCash receipt anyway: the page reads the amount, and if it’s short it tells you exactly how much is left to pay with the same QR. Paid too much? Message us on Facebook and we’ll sort it out.

      Why does GCash show Hokkaido Ice Cream?

      That’s the name on our GCash account, so it’s the right place to pay. Your GCash receipt shows it too, and that’s the receipt you add on this page.

      Is Purrfect POS BIR-accredited?

      No. It is a register and sales tracker, not a BIR-accredited POS machine, so its receipts say “This is not an official receipt”. Keep issuing your BIR-registered receipts or invoices as usual.

      Which printers work?

      Most Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58 mm and 80 mm) sold on Shopee and Lazada, from Chrome on Android, Windows or Mac. If Chrome can’t find yours, the free RawBT app on Android connects it. On a computer you can also use the normal print dialog.

      Does it work on iPhone?

      Yes for selling, receipts on screen and reports. iPhones can’t talk to Bluetooth printers from a web app, so for printed receipts use an Android phone or tablet, or a computer.

      Where are my sales saved? Can two tablets share them?

      On the device you sell on, never on our servers. Each device keeps its own sales and report. Back up to a file every week, and restore it on a new phone. Need one report across branches or devices? That’s our full system.
