FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once (₱179₱299) and keep using it, with free updates.

Until when is the 40% off?

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The free demo is the full app with sample items; receipts just print “DEMO”. Add your own items there: they stay when you activate.

How soon do I get my license key?

We check each GCash payment by hand, usually within a few hours between 8 AM and 10 PM. The key comes by email and also shows on this page once confirmed.

Is Purrfect POS BIR-accredited?

No. It is a register and sales tracker, not a BIR-accredited POS machine, so its receipts say “This is not an official receipt”. Keep issuing your BIR-registered receipts or invoices as usual.

Which printers work?

Most Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58 mm and 80 mm) sold on Shopee and Lazada, from Chrome on Android, Windows or Mac. If Chrome can’t find yours, the free RawBT app on Android connects it. On a computer you can also use the normal print dialog.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes for selling, receipts on screen and reports. iPhones can’t talk to Bluetooth printers from a web app, so for printed receipts use an Android phone or tablet, or a computer.

Where are my sales saved? Can two tablets share them?

On the device you sell on, never on our servers. Each device keeps its own sales and report. Back up to a file every week, and restore it on a new phone. Need one report across branches or devices? That’s our full system.

I changed phones or lost my key.

Your key is in the email we sent; it works on up to 3 devices. Message us on Facebook if you need it moved to a new device.
