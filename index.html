FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

My business isn’t a café. Can you still help?

Yes. If customers ask, book, order or pay, we can digitalize it: commercial spaces and offices, apartments and boarding houses, staycations, salons and clinics, shops, laundries, events, classes and more. Tell us how you work today and we’ll show you what changes.

Can you handle leasing for a building with many units?

Yes. Each unit gets its own listing, viewing schedule, lease, billing and repair history, and the owner sees occupancy and collections for the whole building in one report. Pro covers multiple buildings.

Do I need to buy new equipment?

No. Everything runs on a phone, tablet or laptop you already have. A receipt printer is optional.

What if my staff aren’t techy?

We train them on site during week four and check in daily for two weeks after handover. The screens use big buttons and plain words.

Who owns the data?

You do. Sheets, pages and Messenger settings are created under your own accounts from day one.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with Starter and move up any time. You only pay the difference in setup fees.

Where do you work?

We start in Cavite (Dasmariñas, Bacoor, Imus) so we can visit in person. Remote setup elsewhere is possible; ask us.

How do I pay?

Half of the setup fee at kickoff and half at handover. The care plan is billed monthly.
