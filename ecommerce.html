FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Is the Founding Seller pilot really free?

Yes. We set up the starter tools at no cost and you pay nothing during the pilot. In return we ask for honest feedback and permission to share your results as a case study. You only pay if you decide to move to Seller Growth or the full ecosystem.

How do you connect to TikTok Shop, Shopee and Lazada?

Through each platform’s official seller tools and order exports, or through a multichannel tool you already use. We set up whatever fits your volume and budget, and the data stays in accounts you own.

Will this stop overselling during big sales?

That is the main job of the stock sync. One count is shared by every shop, and items are held as soon as an order comes in on any platform.

Can you handle our chats for us?

Yes, through managed customer service. Our agents reply from the same inbox using your saved answers, and you see every conversation.

We use a 3PL warehouse. Does that still work?

Yes. Orders and stock flow to and from your 3PL, and you still see packing, shipping and returns in one place.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks for the Founding Seller pilot and four weeks for the full ecosystem, including training your team.
